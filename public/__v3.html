Our principles

Four things we refuse to compromise on.

I

Privacy is a right, not a setting.

If protecting a child means watching them, we’ve built it wrong.

II

A guardian’s job isn’t just to prevent harm — it’s to help a child become more.

III

Presence, not surveillance.

We build companions that stay close, not systems that look on from a distance.

IV

On-device by design.

Trust shouldn’t depend on a promise. It should be built into the architecture.
